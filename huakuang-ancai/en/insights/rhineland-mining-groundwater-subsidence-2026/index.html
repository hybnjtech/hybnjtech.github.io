Mining impacts extend beyond pit boundaries

Satellite radar connects groundwater decline with subsidence and fault movement.

Real photograph · thematic illustration
Photo: Raimond Spekking · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Real view of Garzweiler lignite mine, Germany · Resized and cropped for display

Two studies reported by GFZ link falling groundwater in Germany’s Rhenish lignite district to widespread subsidence and slow fault reactivation. Satellite radar and field observations reveal deformation risks beyond pit boundaries.
