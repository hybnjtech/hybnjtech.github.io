HKA Sans is a web subset of Noto Sans SC, distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Original source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
Original font: NotoSansSC[wght].ttf
Modifications (2026-11-04): subset Latin, Chinese, Japanese kana and punctuation; remove hinting; restrict weight axis to 400-700; convert to WOFF2; rename family to HKA Sans.
The full original copyright and license are preserved in NotoSansSC-OFL.txt.

Mirror edition: further subset to glyphs in the current public snapshot. Regenerated on each export.
